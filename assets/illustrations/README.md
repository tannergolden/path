## 💡 What This Folder Is For

Drawn artwork: the hero image at the top of a page, the art for an empty state,
a spot illustration beside a feature, a mascot. SVG wherever the art allows it,
with a `-dark` twin when it sits on the page's background.

Keep the layered source (Figma, Illustrator, Affinity) beside the export, or
link it in the log. Artwork that was bought or commissioned names its artist and
its licence in its row.
