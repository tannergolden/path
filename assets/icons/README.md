## 💡 What This Folder Is For

Small symbols the documents use: the icon beside a feature, the logo of a
platform the project works with, the mark on a step. SVG on a square grid, from
one set with one stroke weight, so they read as a family. The project's own icon
belongs in [`../branding/`](../branding/README.md).

A third-party logo is used under its owner's brand guidelines, never altered or
recoloured beyond what they allow. Link the guidelines in its row.
